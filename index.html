ABOUT YOUR TUTOR

Hi, I'm ALEX

I tutor the LSAT with one main goal: helping students actually understand the reasoning behind the test instead of relying on a giant collection of shortcuts, tricks, and rules they have memorized.

My approach focuses on figuring out exactly how you think through a question, identifying where that reasoning starts to break down, and building a process that you can repeat consistently across Logical Reasoning and Reading Comprehension.

LSAT prep is not one-size-fits-all. Different students struggle for different reasons, which is why tutoring should adapt to the student rather than forcing the student to adapt to a rigid curriculum.
